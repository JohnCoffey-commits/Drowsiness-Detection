# Stage 15 Figure Title Fix

## Issue

The Stage 15 fusion figures were generated by reusing the Stage 13 plotting helper. As a result, the timeline figure titles said:

```text
Stage 13 F5 Fusion Timeline: ...
```

This was misleading because the figures are Stage 15 real synchronized mouth-eye fusion validation outputs.

## Fix

Updated:

- `src/runtime/stage15_real_mouth_eye_fusion_validation.py`

The Stage 15 wrapper now owns Stage 15-specific plotting helpers:

- `plot_stage15_state_counts`
- `plot_stage15_video_timeline`

The timeline title now uses:

```text
Stage 15 F5 Real Mouth-Eye Fusion Timeline: ...
```

The state-count title now uses:

```text
Stage 15 F5 Real Mouth-Eye Fusion State Counts by Video
```

## Fusion Logic

No fusion logic changed.

No thresholds changed.

No input timelines changed.

No numeric outputs changed intentionally; the Stage 15 wrapper was rerun only to regenerate figures and reports with corrected labels.

- `outputs/stage15_real_mouth_eye_fusion/figures/fusion_state_counts_by_video.png`
- `outputs/stage15_real_mouth_eye_fusion/figures/fusion_timeline_A_normal_open_baseline.png`
